Timing, filtering and label quality

EMG time is reconstructed from the sample counter on the browser’s monotonic timeline. Camera labels use frame-callback timestamps. These are estimates, not hardware synchronization. Positive label delay aligns an EMG window with a later visual label; determine this using training recordings only.

The default waveform filter is causal Butterworth SOS, with a narrow optional mains notch. No common-average subtraction is applied. The first second after startup or a packet gap is excluded from training. Digital filtering cannot restore EMG bandwidth above this hardware’s Nyquist limit.

MediaPipe’s handedness score is not a landmark confidence score. Labels are rejected for missing or ambiguous hands, frame-edge clipping, degenerate geometry, implausible jumps, and missing pose calibration. Monocular flexion remains an estimate.
