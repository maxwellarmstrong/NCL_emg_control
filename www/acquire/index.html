    Timing, filtering and label quality

    EMG time is reconstructed from the sample counter on the browser’s monotonic timeline. Camera labels use frame-callback timestamps. These are estimates, not hardware synchronization. Positive label delay aligns an EMG window with a later visual label; determine this using training recordings only.

    The default waveform filter is causal Butterworth SOS, with a narrow optional mains notch. No common-average subtraction is applied. Startup, packet/time gaps, ADC clipping and uncertain timing restart the filter; the first second of clean recovery is excluded from training. Amber traces show settling, blank breaks show missing or rejected samples. The display preserves per-pixel peaks. Y-axis changes affect only the display, never saved samples or CNN inputs. Digital filtering cannot restore EMG bandwidth above this hardware’s Nyquist limit.

    MediaPipe’s handedness score is not a landmark confidence score. Labels are rejected for missing or ambiguous hands, frame-edge clipping, degenerate geometry, implausible jumps, and missing pose calibration. Arm tracking separately checks shoulder, elbow and wrist visibility. Elbow and upper-arm-to-torso angles come from pose world coordinates; they are monocular estimates, not calibrated motion capture. Arm and hand coordinate origins differ and are never joined in 3D. The CNN still learns five finger targets; arm pose is saved alongside them.
